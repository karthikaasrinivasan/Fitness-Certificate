  FC 1 - Main Auditorium

1. Is the main auditorium adequately equipped with modern audiovisual technology for teaching purposes?

2. Does the main auditorium provide sufficient seating capacity for conducting lectures and seminars?

3. Are the acoustics of the main auditorium conducive to clear communication during lectures?

4. Is the lighting in the main auditorium suitable for creating an optimal learning environment?

5. Are the facilities in the main auditorium well-maintained and in good condition?

6. Does the layout of the main auditorium facilitate student engagement and interaction during classes?

7. Is the main auditorium well-ventilated to ensure fresh air circulation?

8. Is the main auditorium equipped with climate control systems to maintain a comfortable temperature?

9. Is the main auditorium equipped with comfortable seating?

10. Is the main auditorium equipped with projection screens for presentations?

11. Are the flooring materials in the main auditorium intact and free from damage or tripping hazards?

12. Are the restroom facilities adjacent to the main auditorium clean, functional, and well-stocked with necessary supplies?

13. Are the audiovisual systems in the main auditorium fully operational and free from technical issues?

14. Is the main auditorium's stage area clean and well-maintained, with no signs of damage or deterioration?

15. Are the emergency exits in the main auditorium clearly marked and easily accessible in case of evacuation?
